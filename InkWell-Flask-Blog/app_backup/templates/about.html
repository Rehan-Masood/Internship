About

A quiet place to write things worth reading.

Inkwell is a small publishing platform built for writers who want a clean, distraction-free space to share ideas. Create an account, write an article, and start a conversation in the comments.

Built with Flask, SQLAlchemy, Flask-Login, and WTForms — a full authentication system, CRUD-based article management, threaded comments, and profile picture uploads.
